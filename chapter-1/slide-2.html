Benefits and Potential Harms of Screening

Benefits

  • ✓Finding cancer early

    Screening can detect breast cancer before a lump can be felt or symptoms develop.

  • ✓Less treatment

    When cancer is found early, it is often smaller and easier to treat. This may allow for less extensive surgery and fewer treatments.

  • ✓Better outcomes

    Early detection can lower risk of dying from cancer.

Potential Harms

  • ⚠False positives

    A false positive is when a screening test shows something that looks like cancer, but turns out not to be cancer. Think of it like a broken fire alarm that goes off when there is no fire.

  • ⚠Extra tests

    If something unusual appears on a screening test, your doctor may recommend more imaging or a biopsy. These tests often show no cancer, but the process can cause worry and discomfort.
