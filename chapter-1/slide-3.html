False Positives

A false positive is when a screening test flags an abnormality that needs further workup but turns out not to be cancer.

Think of it like a fire alarm when there is no fire!

  • For women who got MRIs, 10 out of 100 patients will need further testing and most of those will be benign. These are false positives.
  • Extra tests can lead to more worry, cost, discomfort. The benefits often outweigh the risks
