Key Takeaways and Action Items

  • ★The main purpose of screening is to detect breast cancer early.
  • ★Screening can detect cancer before symptoms develop.
  • ★The benefits of screening (early detection, less treatment, better outcomes) need to be weighed against the harms (false positive results, biopsies, over diagnosis).
