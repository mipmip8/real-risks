Key Takeaways and Action Items

  • ★Mammograms are the mainstay of breast cancer screening for early detection.
  • ★For women at high-risk for breast cancer, breast MRI should be used for supplemental screening.
  • ★For women with dense breasts, breast ultrasound or contrast-enhanced mammogram may be used for supplemental screening.
  • ★After an abnormal screening test, a biopsy may be needed to confirm whether breast cancer is present.
