Breast Biopsy30–60 min

What to expect:

  1. The area is cleaned and numbed with local anesthesia (this feels similar to when you go to the dentist and they use numbing gel)
    1. For lumps that can be seen with ultrasound, under ultrasound guidance, a hollow needle is inserted to remove small cores of tissue
    2. For findings that can only be seen with mammography or contrast-enhanced mammography, under X-ray guidance a hollow needle is inserted to remove small cores of tissue
    3. For findings only seen on breast MRI under MRI guidance a hollow needle is inserted to remove small cores of tissue.
  2. The samples are sent to the lab to be examined under the microscope by a pathologist
  3. The findings usually take 5-10 business days to result, but sometimes can take longer
