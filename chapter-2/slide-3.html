Mammogram~15 min

  1. Preparation

    • No deodorant, perfume, or lotion on chest or underarms
    • Remove jewelry
    • Change into a gown (top off)

  2. Positioning

    • Stand in front of the machine
    • One breast is placed on a flat plate
    • The technologist will help guide you

  3. Imaging

    • The breast is compressed between two plates for pictures at different angles
    • X-ray images are taken
    • They may reposition you
    • This is repeated for the other breast
