What happens at an MRI appointment

A breast MRI has more steps than most scans, and not knowing them is part of what makes it daunting. Walk through an appointment here, one step at a time.

  1. In the waiting room You are handed a gown and asked to take your jewellery off. You are still in your own clothes at this point — nothing happens to you in the waiting room.
  2. In the changing room Jewellery off, gown on. An MRI works with magnetic fields, and it cannot be performed if you have certain types of metal in your body, so metal stays out of the scan room.
  3. The IV goes in The IV is in, placed by the technologist. It is how the contrast dye will reach you later. Some people are allergic to contrast dye, so tell your doctor if you have reacted to it before.
  4. Onto the table You are on the table, face down. A breast MRI is done lying on your stomach, and the technologist is there to help you on.
  5. The technologist adjusts you In position. The technologist checks you and makes small adjustments before anything moves.
  6. Take the safety button You have it. You can press it at any point during the scan if you need the staff.
  7. Into the machine You are inside, and the technologist steps out of the room before the scan begins. The machine can feel loud, narrow or uncomfortable for some people — worth saying beforehand if you think it might for you.
  8. The scan You will hear loud tapping noises. You are inside the tunnel for about 15 to 20 minutes.
