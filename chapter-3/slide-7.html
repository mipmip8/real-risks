Paying for Screening

  • Insurance often covers screening testsMany health plans cover routine mammograms at no cost to you—check with your provider to be sure.
  • Costs may vary for follow-up testsIf additional imaging or tests are needed, you may have out-of-pocket costs like copays or deductibles. Copays may be high for expensive studies like MRIs.
  • Call your insurance to confirm coverageAsk what is covered, which facilities are in-network, and whether you need a referral.
