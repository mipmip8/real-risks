Key Takeaways and Action Items

  • ★Screening recommendations may vary among individuals based upon breast cancer risk.
  • ★Compared to average-risk people, those at high-risk for breast cancer may need to start screening at a younger age, more frequently, and with supplemental screening tests, such as breast MRI, in addition to mammogram.
  • ★Knowing your breast cancer risk can personalize your screening recommendations (see Module 1 on Breast Cancer Risk).
