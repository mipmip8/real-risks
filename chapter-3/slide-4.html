Breast Cancer Risk Categories

Average Risk Women

All of the following must be true:

  • No personal history of breast cancer or atypia on biopsy
  • Do not carry a high risk genetic mutation (such as BRCA1 or BRCA2)
  • No strong family history of breast or ovarian cancer

High Risk Women

If any of the following are true:

  • Have a known breast cancer risk gene change based on genetic testing
  • Have a strong family history of breast or ovarian cancer
  • Have a lifetime risk of breast cancer of about 20-25% or greater
  • Have had a breast biopsy with atypia
  • Have had chest radiation for cancer treatment before age 30
